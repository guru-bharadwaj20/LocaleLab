# Code of conduct

This project is small and I would like it to stay pleasant. These are the rules for issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that belongs to the project.

## Expected

- Be direct about code and kind about people. "This regex misses the ICU plural case" helps. "Who wrote this" does not.
- Assume the other person is busy and acting in good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Give credit, including for bug reports and failed experiments.
- Keep discussions on the work. Personal characteristics, politics and off-topic arguments do not belong here.

## Not accepted

- Insults, slurs, or demeaning remarks about anyone, including people outside the project.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or private, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, repeating a closed argument, or spamming.

## Scope

The rules apply in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project, for example when using a project email address or speaking on its behalf at an event.

## Reporting

Write to gururb20@gmail.com. Include links or screenshots if you have them. I will read every report, keep the reporter's identity private as far as the situation allows, and reply within a week with what I intend to do.

## Consequences

Depending on severity, the response is a private warning, a request to edit or remove content, a temporary block from the project's spaces, or a permanent ban. Anyone asked to stop a behaviour is expected to stop immediately.
